Property Authority Required for Solar
Solar installation needs sign-off from whoever owns the property. Until that permission is in hand we can't proceed, and that protects you as much as the owner.
  • Panels are a permanent structural modification to the roof
  • We require documented permission before any work begins
  • Getting that permission is often simpler than people expect
Once you have that permission in writing, come back and complete this assessment. Plenty of people install solar successfully on a property they don't own.
